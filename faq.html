Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our team, projects and how to get in touch.

Who are BONNY AND CLYDE?

We are a pair of Computer Science students learning and building small projects for the web.

What technologies do you use?

Our work uses HTML for page structure, CSS for presentation and JavaScript for interactive features.

How can I browse your projects?

Visit the Projects page and use its buttons to filter projects by HTML, CSS, JavaScript or Git.

How can I contact the team?

Use the Contact page to write us a message. The message needs at least 20 characters before the form accepts it.
